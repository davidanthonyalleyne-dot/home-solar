What's new in 1.11.1
- The day view's inside and outside charts are now one Temperatures chart: inside, the thermostat setting and outside on the same scale.
- Each line is labelled once at its right end, so labels no longer sit on top of the lines.
- Touch and drag across the chart to read inside, set point and outside for any hour.
- "Show as table" lists all three every two hours.
